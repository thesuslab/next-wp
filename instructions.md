Positioning

Sustainability Lab

The experience should communicate:

experimental enough for entrepreneurs
intelligent enough for technology people

visually distinctive enough to feel like a lab, not an NGO template

30. Visual Design System
Overall aesthetic

Think:

research institute × technology lab × architectural studio × cultural workshop

Not:

eco NGO × corporate consultancy

Typography

Use one expressive display face and one highly readable grotesk.

Headlines

Large.

Confident.

Occasionally awkwardly oversized.

Example:

MAKE
THE FUTURE
LESS FRAGILE.

Body

Simple, highly readable.

Avoid excessive rounded SaaS typography.

39. The brand should ultimately say this without saying it

When someone leaves the website, I want them thinking:

"These people aren't just talking about sustainability. They're building an ecosystem around it."

That's the distinction I'd protect.

The consultancy work gives Sustainability Lab credibility.

The AI tools give it scalability.

KÃRVA gives it physical/tangible expression.

The coworking space gives it community.

The partnership model gives it reach.

And the Lab itself becomes the umbrella connecting all four.
